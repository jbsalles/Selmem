# Active experiment: LoCoMo strategy

The active experiment uses one observed conversation, two salience interventions,
matched LLM questions, donor-context copies, nosleep, null and raw-source controls.
The memory organ is frozen. Its aim is to test context-dependent advice and
creative design, with factual controls; individuality and creativity gains remain
unproven.

- [Protocol, configuration and local commands](docs/LOCOMO_STRATEGY.md)
- [Retained Grok and GPT observations](experiments/RESULTS.md)
- [Frozen organ baseline](docs/ORGAN_AUDIT_FIXES.md)
- [Experiment index and historical benchmarks](experiments/README.md)

The full comparison has ten conditions and six probes. Three repetitions cost
180 requested calls; five cost 300. Dry runs make zero provider calls. The old
LoCoMo futures protocols and earlier strategy pilots are retired. `--wow` is
accepted for command compatibility but no longer selects a separate mode.

The older stake, persist, horizon and other benches remain historical evidence
and regression fixtures. They are not revisions or results of this experiment.
