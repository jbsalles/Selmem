# GENESIS long trajectories — economical design 3

This is a reduced-cost version of the long experiment, not a new short scientific pilot. Every retained history still has all its source sessions, 90 exposure days and 30 washout days. The Rust organ and experimental controls remain active. The experiment engine, data preparation, and rating tools use Rust; Bash launchers require no JavaScript or Python runtime.

## Predeclared scope

The economy fixture uses the first two complete conversations from the pinned ten-conversation LoCoMo text release: 788 source turns. Each defines a paired A/B history with a common first-session prefix. Remaining turns are spread chronologically over 90 virtual days. Source timestamps and speaker wrappers are retained metadata, not elapsed autobiographical years. Images, captions, summaries and QA answers are excluded.

Each branch has four native architectures: full, an independent full replica with identical realized exposure, no-sleep and lifetime no-reconstruction/reconsolidation. There are 16 primary lives. No profile threshold, admission rule or sleep budget is lowered to manufacture a result. The full memory chooses among a joint trial, a written plan and independent inspection in six rotating practical settings. Other architectures receive the same realized actions and outcomes. Trial/plan reliability is 80/20 in one branch and reversed in the other; inspection is 50%. The favored approach reverses after day 60. Seeded draws depend on pair/day/choice, not branch. Reliability labels and unobserved potential outcomes are audit-only.

Concrete minor observations, joyful recognition, public failure and counterexamples are supplied without numeric salience, personality, dogma or clinical trauma labels. Same-day action, result and extra event share an observation identity. Native gate/interpretation decide what remains. Event identity prevents counting fragments as independent support, but independence of situations still requires human audit.

All architectures have daily live reflection during exposure. Full, replica and no-reconstruction sleep normally; no-sleep advances the same clock. The following 30 days have no new input or reflection for primary lives, retaining their assigned sleep treatment. Choices, reconstructions and native mutations remain independent operations; only stateless interpretation of identical text is shared.

## Evaluation

Checkpoints are day 0, 90 and 120; the intermediate day-30 evaluation is removed. At each checkpoint, three identical English tasks concern museum navigation across languages, ferry allocation with private needs and school water-retention discovery. Seven conditions remain: full, same-history replica, reconstruction disabled only at the readonly probe, no-sleep, lifetime no-reconstruction, faithful BM25 RAG and empty context. Selection IDs must match between full and probe-only reconstruction ablation.

Primary cells have two creative generation repeats. Final structural interventions and latent continuations have one repeat per task/cell. All use the same model, memory/choice temperature zero, creative temperature 0.7 and paired requested seeds; providers may ignore seeds. Choices have a 64-output-token cap and creative answers 400, in addition to the 180-word instruction. Truncation or invalid output is retained as failure, not resampled. These caps may themselves cause a format failure and must be reported.

Native context contains the organ's actual query-scoped reading, available axioms/mood and recalled narratives at a 600 whole-entry-word maximum. No invented persona or sealed archive is supplied. Native contexts can be empty; unexposed beliefs cannot be assumed to influence generation. Transfer remains the existing explicit topical mode with redundancy penalty and unchanged eligibility thresholds, separate from ordinary factual retrieval. Word maxima do not imply identical facts, information volume or token counts across native/RAG conditions.

All six final interventions remain: intact, remove beliefs, remove co-recall links, remove latent traces, reset current mood and remove nonlatent episodes while retaining residual structures. Record actual availability, before/after states, removed IDs and supplied context. A separate continuation starts at day 90: intact and latent-removal copies receive the same six later neutral events and 30 nights, with removal before those events. Missing layers or ineffective removals are observations, not evidence against an unactivated mechanism. Removing traces can remove incident associations and have multiple mediated effects; this is not a uniquely identified psychological cause or proof of unconsciousness.

## Shared annotation cache

Previously each architecture could pay for reinterpreting identical external text. In economical design 3, only a successfully parsed and validated stateless semantic response is cached. Its key contains the complete endpoint and request body: model, reasoning, temperature, prompt/schema and event text. Responses have a checksum, are revalidated when reused and are persisted across resumption. A changed event, model, endpoint or reasoning produces a different entry. Invalid replies are not persisted. Cache corruption aborts the operation without committing the life.

This shares elementary perception of identical text across conditions. Variability of the annotator itself is no longer estimated; conclusions are conditional on these shared annotations. State-dependent memory reconstruction, sleep narration, choices, scorings and creative generations are not shared by this cache. The full replica therefore still provides developmental variability beyond independent creative-generation repeats. This application cache is distinct from provider prompt caching and avoids whole HTTP requests, not merely some input-token charges.

There are 829 distinct possible full event texts over these two complete source histories, all potential action/outcome combinations and continuation events. This is not a bound on all semantic calls: splitting can add new part texts and failed uncached replies can be retried. The actual number of cache hits, misses and HTTP attempts is recorded. Count costs from actual HTTP exchanges once, not from the original usage metadata repeated in cache-hit audit records.

## Measurable work reduction

| Item | Full design 2 | Economical design 3 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Source pairs | 10 | 2 |
| Primary lives | 80 | 16 |
| Source turns | 5,882 | 788 |
| Exposure + washout days per life | 90 + 30 | 90 + 30 |
| Native event encodes including continuations | 60,448 | 8,974 |
| Native nights including continuations | 8,400 | 1,680 |
| Choice generations | 1,800 | 360 |
| Primary creative proposals | 5,040 | 504 |
| Structural-intervention proposals | 1,080 | 72 |
| Continuation proposals | 360 | 24 |
| Total creative proposals | 6,480 | 600 |
| Default cumulative HTTP ceiling | 200,000 | 12,000 |

The exact total HTTP count and real duration remain unmeasured. Reconstructions, sleep and scoring add state-dependent calls. The 12,000-attempt ceiling pauses the run if reached and does not guarantee completion or impose a dollar/time cap. Retry attempts and resumed work share it. A higher explicit ceiling is logged as an amendment. Actual per-request tokens and provider billing determine cost.

For comparison only, the earlier hypothetical mean of 1,000 input plus 200 output tokens at $1.25/$2.50 per million gives $8.75 for 5,000 calls, $21 for 12,000. At a hypothetical 10 seconds per call these are about 14 and 33 hours, before retry waits and local work. These are scenarios, not measured forecasts or guaranteed budgets; actual lengths and rates may differ.

## Persistence and scientific limits

Copy-on-write life heads, durable committed receipts, state hashes, immutable checkpoints, replayed operations, cached decisions/generations and run locking remain. Do not edit/pull sources, switch runtime/model/effort or alter fixtures during collection. The new binary cannot resume an older binary's run. Start a fresh output directory. A lost reply before persistence can still cause a paid retry; a validated annotation already saved can be reused if a later mutation fails.

Two source pairs are a case study, not population-level validation. Repeated tasks, dates, conditions and generated samples are nested observations, not independent N. Reducing generation repeats and using one per lesion weakens estimates of intervention robustness. No novelty, belief, creativity advantage or unconscious process follows from lexical/tag distance or mock tests.

Give only blind-proposals.json to at least two independent raters, preserving utility/originality axes, constraint compliance, rationales and disagreement. The exploratory joint measure is min(utility, originality), zero for invalid/constraint-failed answers; it is not a validated creativity scale. Lock ratings before source inspection. Separate functional coding reports consensus, disagreements, A/B differences, within-history generation variation and same-history replica variation. It does not prove creativity.

A post-selection source audit can still request the five-arm causal follow-up (intact, A, B, AB, two unselected sham roots), three paired repeats, 15 proposals per audited case. Two distinct known observation roots must have been shown; independence and sham comparability require human audit. This follow-up is not run automatically and has its own ceiling. Derived structures may retain source information after removal.

The earlier high-load state-integrity failure is not declared diagnosed or resolved by this cost optimization. Assertions remain active. The original full fixture from package 003 remains accepted for a new full-mode run, with its old scale and 200,000-attempt default; full mode does not enable the new shared annotation cache. Do not use it for the reduced-cost run.
