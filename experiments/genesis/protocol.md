# GENESIS protocol and interpretation

## Current operational hypothesis

Cumulative observations can affect decisions when memory retains their evidential multiplicity. This is narrower than a claim of individuality or creativity. The current experiments qualify that chain before investigating stronger claims.

The positive control provides faithful external observations under explicit simulator rules: independent reports from a stationary unknown accuracy, a Beta(1,1) prior, and predictive accuracy `(successes+1)/(successes+failures+2)`. Following yields NET `20p-10`; verification guarantees NET 7 or 3; alternative guarantees NET 4. Twelve successes favor following at both costs. Failures/empty history favor verification at the low cost and alternative at the high cost. The oracle and shuffled schedule are saved before calls.

Learning offers the same Neri observations through ordinary native intake and recall. Observation identity is explicit metadata (`event.id`), outside the factual sentence. Two fresh lives receive reliable/unreliable histories. The OFF/ON cut changes only support activation within this revised representation. No significance is assigned to names or labels, and no preferred action is encoded into the organ.

Readouts run on independent identical snapshot clones under native/strict reconstruction. Strict can return core after an adverse scorer label. Each readout is frozen for subsequent decisions; probes never alter the source life. Full external history and admitted-only external observations are controls, not runtime archive retrieval. No synthetic counts are supplied by the orchestrator: support counts come from the organ's identified observations.

## Measures

Report intake outcomes separately from trace creation. Compare full faithful to admitted-faithful history to identify admission loss, admitted-faithful to actual recall to identify access/reconstruction loss, and native to strict for factual checking. Compare choice and performance separately. A full-history oracle score can penalize a reasonable choice made with reduced evidence; inspect what was actually supplied.

Invalid structured answers, budgets and API failures retain partial reports. No semantic retries force admission. Report all histories and failed controls. Model/evaluator competence must be independently checked. Raw response version/fingerprint and requested temperature are retained where available; temperature zero is not guaranteed deterministic generation.

## Limits

One pair, fixed cut order, one final generated recall per policy/history, and repeated frozen decision contexts are exploratory. Counts of distinct caller IDs do not prove statistical independence outside the controlled fixture. Explicit reliability rules qualify arithmetic and input use. They do not demonstrate spontaneous personality. Support metadata can explain the entire observed effect without any contribution from reconstruction or co-recall.

The default ordinary organ keeps observation support disabled; the paired assay explicitly enables/disables it. Exact repeats accumulate conservatively. Counter-observation matching depends on an external scorer; unknown relations remain on the salience path. Saturation is bounded, and counts do not transfer through merged traces. Current runs have no sleep and cannot establish long-term consolidation.

## Stronger hypotheses, not yet demonstrated

| Hypothesis | Required next evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Causal support contribution | Lesion support on clones while preserving other state; sham intervention and source hash checks |
| Revision | Mixed and reversing observations, separately counted contradictions, calibrated decision adaptation |
| Persistence | Shared neutral episodes and prespecified horizons on independent lives |
| Singularity | Reproducible history-dependent choices on new tasks/actors beyond identical-history sham divergence |
| Memory causality | Behavior follows transplanted state and changes under targeted/combined lesions |
| Reconstructive specificity | Matched-budget comparison with faithful history, RAG, summary, static reconstruction and no-memory generation |
| Creativity | Executable designs scored for utility, structural/behavioral novelty and causal historical dependence on unseen worlds |

Prespecify meaningful effects after estimating natural variability; separate development from confirmation and compute uncertainty over independent histories. A null effect, reduced performance or rigidity can refute the proposed benefit. Do not select only dramatic outputs or treat lexical variation as behavior.

## Matched sleep intervention

[SLEEP.md](SLEEP.md) specifies a paired pilot at 0/1/5/20 cycles, with identical prefix snapshots, equal virtual time, source hashes and isolated probes. Controlled decision utility and LoCoMo-derived factual correctness remain separate measures. This tests preservation and use of memory after sleep, not creative superiority.

## Creative transfer extension

See [CREATIVITY.md](CREATIVITY.md) for the executable exploratory LoCoMo-derived design. The collection has four conditions sharing a task and model; native full/no-reconstruction share one saved life. Blind utility/originality ratings precede source audits and predeclared target/sham lesions. No sleep or co-recall learning benefit is tested in this first campaign. Generation repeats are nested within histories and do not establish population-level individuality or creativity.
