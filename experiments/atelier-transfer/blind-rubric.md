# Rating the workshop commissions

Make your own copy of `blind.json` and keep each proposal ID. Read the commission
and proposal. Enter an integer from 1 to 5 in both quality fields before coding
the choice of device.

| Field | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `originality` | The central artistic choice is predictable | It makes a recognizable device work in a distinct way | Its central device or combination is surprising and intelligible |
| `fit` | The design misses the purpose or an essential constraint | It broadly works and needs revision | The audience could perceive the intended effect and the design meets every stated constraint |

In `device`, record the central means by which the proposal makes the audience
notice the intended moment or place. Use one of these exact values.

| Value | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| `interruption` | A break, removal or absence in a pattern carries the effect. |
| `concentration` | Bunching, layering or locally increasing the number or frequency of repeated elements carries the effect. |
| `both` | Both devices are central to the same intended effect. |
| `other` | Another device carries the effect, or the proposal gives no clear means. |

Use the actual device in the proposal. An ordinary pause or a group of objects
does not qualify unless that feature is how the proposal makes the moment or
place stand out. Code the device independently of whether you liked the proposal.

Complete your own copy before discussing the proposals with the other rater.
